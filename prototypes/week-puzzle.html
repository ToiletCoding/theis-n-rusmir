Drag a piece onto the board. The day letters on a piece choose which days Optimize may use for it. Click a placed piece to pin it. Changing a length also resizes unpinned pieces of that kind where there's room.

Optimize keeps pinned pieces and any unpinned piece that already follows the rules. It moves the ones that don't, then adds pieces until each target is met. A ! marks a piece that breaks a rule. The rules:

  • Study only between 12 and 17
  • Nothing in time that has already passed

Days since

Count the days since something started: a habit, a project, a streak. Counters are separate from the week board, targets and Optimize.

Time frame

The hours the board shows each day. Pieces keep their times when you change it.

Gym split

One session per line, in order. Names follow the order of Gym pieces through the week (Monday first), so moving or removing one can rename the ones after it. Gym pieces beyond the split are called Extra Gym. The Gym target always matches the number of sessions. With a split, Gym can land on back-to-back days (still one a day). Leave it empty for no split.

Daily limits

How much Optimize may plan in one day. Work and everything else have separate limits, and the meter under each day shows both.
